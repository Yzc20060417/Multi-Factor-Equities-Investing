# Multi-Factor Strategy: Overview & Pipeline

## 1. Strategy Overview
The objective is to systematically identify alpha sources, combine them into a robust composite signal, and construct an equity portfolio that outperforms the benchmark (e.g. The index). The pipeline is modular, allowing for independent testing of data processing, factor generation, and execution logic.

## 2. Pipeline Architecture

The strategy workflow is divided into **7 key stages**, detailed below:

### Phase 1: Data Acquisition
We begin by establishing a high-quality data foundation.
* **Universe:** Equity pool from index/manually chosen.
* **Data Frequency:** Daily data (roughly 7-8 years time frame is really sufficient, 3-4 years would do).
* **Fields:** Open, Close, Volume, Returns, Turnover Rate, etc.
* **Source:** Bloomberg Terminal/Refinitiv Workspace (If you have access).

### Phase 2: Factor Construction
Factors are derived from raw market data and financial statements. Here I provided the factors (alphas) from my WorldQuant team.
* **Volume-Price Factors:** Signals based on price momentum, volatility, and liquidity.
* **Fundamental Factors:** Valuation, profitability, and growth metrics.
* **Market Sentiment Factors:** Indicators tracking market emotion and investor sentiment.
* So on...

### Phase 3: Data Pre-Processing
Raw factors must be cleaned to ensure statistical validity before modeling.
* **Winsorization (De-extreming):** Removing or capping outliers to prevent skewed results.
* **Standardization:** Converting factors to Z-scores (mean 0, variance 1) for comparability.
* **Neutralization:** Orthogonalizing factors against market cap and industry sectors to isolate pure alpha.

### Phase 4: Single Factor Testing
Before combination, every factor is rigorously tested for predictive power.
* **IC Analysis:** Information Coefficient (IC) and Rank IC tracking over time.
* **Long-Short Testing:** Performance spread between the top and bottom quantiles.
* **Stratified Analysis:** Examining monotonicity across multiple layers (Longs/Shorts).

### Phase 5: Collinearity & Redundancy Analysis
We analyze correlations to ensure we are not simply stacking identical signals.
* **Factor Return Analysis:** Examining the correlation of returns generated by different factors.
* **Cross-Sectional Correlation:** Checking the correlation of factor values at specific time slices.

### Phase 6: Factor Combination (Composite Signal)
Valid factors are merged into a single score for stock selection using various weighting schemes:
* **Univariate Regression Weighting**
* **Rank Weighting**
* **IC / Rank-IC Weighting**
* **Multivariate Regression Weighting**
* **PCA Decomposition**

### Phase 7: Strategy Construction & Backtesting
The final composite score drives the portfolio logic.
* **Asset Allocation:** Rules for capital distribution.
* **Target Group Selection:** Selecting specific baskets based on factor scores.
* **Rebalancing:** Setting the trading frequency (e.g., weekly/monthly).
* **Strategy Backtest:** Comprehensive review of returns and performance metrics.

Ideally we could split the team with everyone interested and responsible of certain parts of the pipeline based on personal expertise and willingness, some recommendation as follows:

## Team Roles & Capabilities Matrix

### 1. Data Infrastructure & Engineering (Phases 1 & 3)
* **Primary Responsibility:** Ensuring the raw data is accurate, accessible, and clean.
* **Key Capabilities:**
    * **Proficiency in Python/SQL:** For robust data ingestion and local storage management (Considering we do have software engineers in this team, this should be helpful).
    * **Data Wrangling:** Experience with `pandas`/`numpy` for efficient cleaning (handling missing values, outlier detection/Winsorization).
    * **Database Management:** Understanding of schema design for time-series financial data.

### 2. Alpha Research & Factor Construction (Phases 2, 4 & 5)
* **Primary Responsibility:** Deriving signals from noise and validating their predictive power.
* **Key Capabilities:**
    * **Financial Domain Knowledge:** Deep understanding of financial statements (for fundamental factors) and market microstructure (for price/volume factors).
    * **Statistical Analysis:** Strong grasp of correlation, regression, and significance testing.
    * **Economic Sense:** Ability to hypothesize new economic rationales for potential alpha sources.

### 3. Strategy Construction & Portfolio Management (Phases 6 & 7)
* **Primary Responsibility:** Combining signals into a cohesive strategy that balances risk and reward.
* **Key Capabilities:**
    * **Optimization Theory:** Knowledge of convex optimization and portfolio construction techniques for asset allocation.
    * **Risk Management:** Understanding of exposure control, neutralizations, and transaction cost modeling.
    * **Machine Learning:** Familiarity with PCA or multivariate regression for sophisticated factor weighting.
